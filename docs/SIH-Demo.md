# PALASH MITRA: Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 5-7 Minute Demonstration Script
## Problem Statement PS-26042 | Government of Jharkhand
**"One Teacher. Multiple Languages. Every Child Included."**

---

## 🎯 Demonstration Objective
Convince hackathon evaluators and Ministry/State judges that **PALASH MITRA** is not a superficial translation app, but an **offline-first AI pedagogical operating system** solving the severe mother-tongue barrier in Jharkhand primary schools.

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step 5-Minute Presentation Flow

### Minute 0:00 - 1:00 | Context & The Real Problem
- **Pitch**: *"In Jharkhand, a teacher trained in Hindi walks into a Grade 4 classroom where 80% of children speak Santhali as their mother tongue. Concepts like Fractions or Plant anatomy are lost in translation. Moreover, the school has zero internet connectivity and devices have only 2GB RAM. PALASH MITRA bridges this classroom gap."*
- **Visual**: Show the Teacher Dashboard displaying: Teacher Sunita Soren (Khunti), Active Class: Grade 4 Mathematics (Fractions), Language: Santhali (Ol Chiki).

### Minute 1:00 - 2:15 | 🏆 WOW MOMENT #1: Real-Time Classroom Voice Bridge & Live Latency
1. Click **"🎙️ रियल-टाइम कक्षा वाक अनुवाद (Voice Classroom)"**.
2. Hand the microphone to the judge or speak in Hindi:
   > *"बच्चों, आज हम भिन्न सीखेंगे।"*
3. System instantly captures Hindi text, translates to authentic Santhali Ol Chiki (`ᱜᱤᱫᱽᱨᱟᱹ ᱠᱚ, ᱛᱮᱦᱮᱧ ᱫᱚ ᱵᱚᱱ ᱦᱟᱹᱴᱤᱧ ᱵᱚᱱ ᱪᱮᱫ-ᱟ᱾`), and plays synthesized audio.
4. **Point to the Live Latency Card**:
   - $\text{STT: } 390\text{ms} + \text{Translation: } 18\text{ms} + \text{TTS: } 140\text{ms} = \mathbf{1.42\text{ seconds}}$.
   - Highlight: *"Judges, this latency is measured dynamically in runtime, comfortably within our < 3.0s SLA."*

### Minute 2:15 - 3:30 | 🏆 WOW MOMENT #2: Airplane Mode (100% Offline Operation)
1. Turn on the **Airplane Mode / Offline Switch** in the top navbar.
2. Banner turns amber: `● OFFLINE MODE - Local Content Pack Active`.
3. Open **"✨ स्मार्ट टीच (Smart Teach)"** or **"द्विभाषी कार्यपत्रक (Worksheets)"**.
4. Generate a Grade 4 Fractions worksheet with bilingual instructions in Hindi and Ol Chiki.
5. Highlight: *"Open DevTools Network tab—Zero network requests. Everything runs locally on device memory."*

### Minute 3:30 - 4:15 | 🏆 WOW MOMENT #3: AI Personalized Remediation
1. Navigate to **"🎯 उपचारात्मक शिक्षण (Remediation)"**.
2. Case study: Student **Birsa Murmu (Class 4)** has a 35% mastery score in subtraction due to borrowing confusion.
3. Tap **"AI उपचारात्मक योजना जनरेट करें"**.
4. AI diagnoses root cause: place-value misunderstanding and prescribes a concrete hands-on activity:
   > *"Use 10-bundle sticks (Kada / ᱵᱤᱸᱰᱟᱹ) and Santhali mother tongue before returning to symbolic numbers."*
5. Tap **"पुनः मूल्यांकन दर्ज करें"** -> Score updates to 85%!

### Minute 4:15 - 5:00 | 🏆 WOW MOMENT #4: Human-in-the-Loop Teacher Correction
1. Navigate to **"📚 भाषा ज्ञान बैंक व सुधार"**.
2. Search for `भिन्न` or click **"सुधारें"**.
3. Teacher edits the Ol Chiki translation or romanized term to reflect their local regional dialect.
4. Tap **"सुधार सहेजें"**.
5. Return to Voice Classroom and translate again—the corrected term appears instantly from local memory!
6. Explain: *"In low-resource tribal NLP, the teacher is our ground-truth data annotator. When connectivity returns, corrections sync to the State Review Board."*

### Minute 5:00 - 5:45 | 🏆 WOW MOMENT #5: ✨ Smart Teach 1-Click Complete Lesson
1. Tap the large glowing **"✨ स्मार्ट टीच"** button.
2. Select: Grade 4 • Mathematics • Fractions • Village Context.
3. System outputs:
   - Bilingual Learning Objective
   - Local Village Example (Sharing a guava / dividing a farm plot)
   - Teacher Explanation with Simpler Mode
   - Hands-on Paper Folding Activity
   - Visual Steps Diagram
   - Printable Bilingual Worksheet
   - 4 Bilingual Flashcards with Pronunciation
   - Adaptive Quiz & Homework
4. Conclude: *"One Teacher. Multiple Languages. Every Child Included."*
