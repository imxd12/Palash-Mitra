## Phase 2: Pilot Deployment Across Khunti District (Q3 2026)
- Deploy TWA Android app to 150 primary schools in Khunti district.
- Integrate Bhashini API endpoints for live cloud transcription where connectivity exists.
- Expand Santhali vocabulary dictionary from 300 to 5,000 verified curriculum terms in collaboration with Central University of Jharkhand.
- Localized voice recordings by native Santhali community speakers for high-fidelity offline audio packs.

## Phase 3: Multi-Tribal Language Expansion (Q4 2026 - Q1 2027)
- Deploy **Ho (*Warang Chiti*)** language pack across West Singhbhum schools.
- Deploy **Mundari** and **Kurukh** language packs across Ranchi and Gumla districts.
- Introduce Computer-Vision based camera worksheet scanner for handwritten student homework checking.
- Federated Learning: Anonymously train small edge NLP translation models using aggregated teacher corrections without moving student data off local servers.
